Anzvanie · Browser extensions

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Developer: Anzvanie

Your content stays in your browser. Covered extensions process content locally to provide their stated features. They do not send that content to the developer or third parties, use analytics, or sell user data.

1. Scope

This policy applies only to the following browser extension published by Anzvanie, on Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons:

  • Markdown to Backlog Notation — converts Markdown text to Nulab Backlog notation.

Other extensions may be added to this list only if their data practices match this policy. This policy does not cover other Astramyst products or services. For the extensions listed here, this extension-specific policy applies instead of the general Astramyst privacy policy.

2. Content processed and its purpose

Markdown to Backlog Notation processes text you type or paste into the converter and text you explicitly import from a webpage using the selection button or context menu. It uses this content only to convert it, display an approximate preview, and provide the converted result.

Selected text may include personal information depending on what you choose to convert. The extension processes this content locally; the developer does not receive it. The extension does not independently collect your identity, browsing history, location, passwords, payment information, or usage statistics.

3. Local storage and retention

The extension saves your current input as a draft in the browser's local extension storage so it can be restored when reopened. A draft remains until it is replaced, cleared, or the extension's data is removed.

Text imported through the context menu is also stored locally to transfer it to the converter window. This pending selection is removed after it is successfully loaded. If it is not loaded, it may remain in local storage until replaced or the extension's data is removed; selections older than two minutes are not imported automatically.

This storage uses the browser's local extension storage, not its synchronization storage. The extension does not upload these records to a server.

4. Browser permissions

  • contextMenus: provides the right-click action for converting selected text.
  • activeTab and scripting: read the current selection from the active page when you click “Use page selection”. The extension does not modify the webpage.
  • storage: saves the draft and transfers selected text locally between extension views.
  • clipboardWrite: writes converted output to the clipboard when you click “Copy result”. The extension does not read the clipboard.

5. Sharing, tracking, and external links

Covered extensions do not sell, rent, or transfer your content to third parties. They contain no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or remotely executed code. They do not use your content for advertising, creditworthiness decisions, or lending purposes.

Markdown conversion does not require network requests. Links in the preview open only when you choose to follow them. The destination website then receives the normal browser request and is governed by its own privacy policy. External Markdown images are represented as links rather than loaded as images.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can edit or replace your draft at any time. Use “Clear” to clear the current input and saved draft. To remove all locally stored extension data, including any unused pending selection, remove the extension using your browser's extension manager.

Text copied to your system clipboard is managed separately by your operating system and any clipboard history tools you use. Clearing the extension's draft does not clear your clipboard.

7. Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, the information you choose to include in that email will be used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive content. Your email provider processes the message under its own policies.

For questions about this policy, contact anzvanie1510@gmail.com.

8. Policy updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the covered extensions. The effective date above will be revised when the policy changes. Changes to data handling will also be reflected in the relevant store disclosures and any required in-product notices or consent flows.
